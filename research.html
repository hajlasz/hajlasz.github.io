Research

I develop and analyze algorithms for spectral problems with extremely large dimensions. Many of these problems come out of applications in quantum chemistry.

Preprints and Publications

Dimension-independent eigenvalue approximation for sparse matrices

Natalia Hajłasz, Christopher Musco, Jonathan Weare, and Robert J. Webber. arXiv preprint, October 2026.

We analyze sparsified iterative methods used by quantum chemists, establishing convergence guarantees and identifying worst-case limitations of methods that perform well in practice

arXiv abstract · PDF

Sublinear-time Spectral Density Estimation for General Symmetric Matrices

Rajarshi Bhattacharjee, Natalia Hajłasz, Cameron Musco, Christopher Musco, and Apoorv Singh. Preprint in progress.

We study when the eigenvalue distribution of a symmetric matrix can be estimated by querying only a small fraction of its entries.
